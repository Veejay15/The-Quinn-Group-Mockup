Anne & Sean | The Quinn Group wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website to search for homes, learn about our communities, and get in touch with us. We are committed to making myfloridainsider.com accessible and to improving it over time.

Our Standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, the widely recognized standard for accessible websites published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

What We Do

Accessibility is part of how this website is designed, built, and maintained. Our practices include:

  • Text alternatives (alt text) that describe meaningful images.
  • A clear page structure with headings, so screen reader users can navigate by section.
  • Text and button colors chosen for readable contrast.
  • Labels on every form field, so they are announced by assistive technology.
  • Layouts that adapt to phones, tablets, and desktop screens, and that support browser zoom.
  • Links and buttons that can be reached and used with a keyboard.
  • Checking new pages and features for accessibility before they are published.

Third-Party Content

Some features on this website are provided by third parties, including the property search and listing pages, the map on our Contact page, and links to social media platforms. We choose providers with accessibility in mind, but we do not fully control their content. If any part of these features is difficult to use, please contact us, and we will help you get the information you need.
