Questions

Straight answers.

If yours isn't here, call either of us. That's what we're for.

What is my home worth in today's market?

An online estimate is a starting point. We look at recent sales on your street, what's competing right now and your home's condition, then walk you through a price range and the reasoning behind it.

Can I search every listing here, like on Zillow?

Yes. The search on this site covers every MLS listing on the Treasure Coast, with saved searches and new-listing alerts. And when you have a question about a home, you reach Anne or Sean directly.

Can you sell my home if I live out of state?

Yes. Many Treasure Coast owners are seasonal or have already moved. We coordinate access, vendors, showings and closing paperwork so you don't need to be here for every step.

I'm buying with a VA loan. Can you help?

Yes. Sean is a Naval Academy graduate and Navy veteran, and we've written a guide to using a VA loan in Stuart and nearby areas. We'll walk you through what VA buyers should expect from offer to closing.

Which areas do you cover?

Our home market is Martin County, with Stuart, Sewall's Point, Jensen Beach, Palm City and Hobe Sound at its center. We also serve St. Lucie, Palm Beach and Indian River counties.
